Neural Intelligence & Computation Lab (NIC Lab, 神经智能与计算实验室)

Compute Brains. Design Intelligence.
计算大脑，设计智能。

NIC Lab explores the brain's computational principles to build intelligent systems with high application value. We integrate mechanism-driven biophysical modeling with data-driven machine learning methods to synergetically advance both brain science and artificial intelligence.
NIC Lab 致力于探索大脑计算机理，构建具备应用价值的智能系统。我们融合机制驱动的生物物理建模与数据驱动的机器学习方法，协同推动脑科学与人工智能的发展。

Research 研究方向

Mechanism-Driven Modeling
机制驱动建模

Biophysical and circuit-level modeling of neural dynamics to uncover the computational principles of the brain.
从生物物理与神经环路层面解析神经动力学，理解大脑计算的内在机制。

Neuro-Medical AI
神经医学人工智能

Multimodal foundation models and training paradigms for real-world diagnosis and treatment of brain disorders.
面向真实场景，构建多模态基础模型与训练范式，解决脑疾病的诊疗需求。

Brain-inspired AI
类脑智能

Designing artificial intelligence algorithms grounded in the structural and functional principles of the brain.
基于大脑结构与功能原理，开发新型人工智能算法。
